# Массовка вступления: изображения и анимация

Создано встроенным инструментом image_gen для версии 2.7.8. Четыре отдельных листа по восемь фаз. Референс — существующая массовка игры `game/assets/prologue/crowd.png`. Фон удаляется при загрузке, масштаб персонажа фиксирован на весь цикл.

## mustard

Файл: `game/assets/prologue/run-mustard-v2.png`.

Запрос:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Production animation sheet for a 2D game. The reference shows several people: use ONLY the black-haired young man in a mustard yellow hoodie, charcoal trousers, cream sneakers and black backpack. Do not include any woman, red hair or other outfit. Preserve this ONE character's identity, haircut, clothes and detailed painted cartoon art style from the reference across ALL EIGHT frames. Layout: EXACTLY four columns and two rows, 8 full-body sprites; read left to right, top to bottom as one looping RUN cycle. Each cell equal size, same head/body size, same facing RIGHT in side profile, consistent ground line, generous gutters, nothing clipped, no labels or writing. Create real successive frames: 1 long right-foot forward contact, 2 right foot bears weight and left heel folds behind, 3 passing left knee high in front and right leg under body, 4 left-foot forward flight with right heel high behind, 5 long LEFT-foot forward contact, 6 left foot bears weight and right heel folds behind, 7 passing RIGHT knee high in front and left leg underneath, 8 right-foot forward flight preparing frame 1. Rear and front legs exchange positions naturally; do not mirror the character to face left. Arms swing opposite the legs, wrists and knees bend anatomically; preserve natural cloth folds, shoes, bag and hair follow-through. No puppet limbs or rigid two-pose duplicates. Character remains approximately same height except small natural run bounce. SOLID perfectly flat MAGENTA (#ff00ff) background for game chroma key, absolutely no checkerboard, ground, shadows, gradients or grid lines. Widescreen 1792x1024 canvas, 4x2 grid.
```
