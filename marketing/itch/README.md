# Обложка itch.io

Обложка создана встроенным ImageGen для страницы «Гуров — Последний удовл». Изображение: `cover.png`. Референсы — главное меню и сцена погони из версии 2.8.7; сторонние исходные фотографии для этой обложки не использовались. Скриншоты страницы взяты непосредственно из игры.

## Запрос

Use case: ads-marketing. Create one finished itch.io game cover, landscape 315:250 aspect ratio, ideally 1260x1000 pixels. Reference image 1 is the actual game's main menu and establishes the two character designs, the dark navy, teal and warm ivory/gold palette, and the title typography. Reference image 2 shows the same Gurov and Ivan in gameplay; use it to preserve their clothing and recognizable faces, not its HUD or extra character. Make a polished illustrated 2D comedy-platformer poster, faithful to these established character designs. Large stern-but-comic professor Gurov with square black glasses, grey beard, navy jacket, white shirt, dark tie, grey trousers and brown book lunges toward the smaller fleeing Ivan with curly brown hair, black glasses, pink shirt, blue jeans and white sneakers. Ivan looks back in surprise. A small harmless cartoon denture flies between them, with papers swirling through the scene. Stylized university silhouette under a crescent moon behind them. Dynamic readable silhouettes, elegant shapes and warm rim light, crisp faces, no gore. The upper third contains very large, beautifully typeset and perfectly legible Russian title, verbatim: 'ГУРОВ' on the first line and 'ПОСЛЕДНИЙ УДОВЛ' on the second line. Spell УДОВЛ exactly У-Д-О-В-Л. No other text, no buttons, no logos, no UI, no border. Keep all important characters and all title letters inside generous safe margins; the image must read as a tiny store thumbnail. Do not introduce additional characters.
